A Simplified Model

The regulator sets electricity rates, rrr, on electricity demand, or load, lll, which we approximate as being perfectly inelastic. The regulator requires the utility to meet load and chooses rrr such that revenues equal the sum of variable costs, TVC, and an allowable return, sss, on the rate base, BBB.
