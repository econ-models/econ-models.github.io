Model Setting

The model describes renewable developers' location choices as a two-stage decision. A project iii first chooses a balancing authority (market) mmm and then chooses a county jjj within the selected market. Developers choose locations to maximize project profitability, which depends on expected revenues, installation costs, local characteristics, and place-based tax credits.

For project iii locating in county jjj within market mmm, static operating profit in period ttt is:

where pijtp_{ijt}pijt​ is the electricity price, QijtQ_{ijt}Qijt​ is annual electricity generation, and OMijtOM_{ijt}OMijt​ denotes operation and maintenance costs. The net present value of the project is:

Estimation

The model is estimated separately for wind and solar projects using a sequential random-coefficients logit procedure.

First, the authors estimate the county-level choice model. Because some balancing authorities contain hundreds of counties, the full county choice set is computationally costly. For each project, the authors therefore construct a sampled choice set containing the observed chosen county and approximately 25 alternative counties. Alternatives are sampled with higher probabilities when they are similar to the chosen county in renewable capacity factors, levelized costs, agricultural characteristics, terrain, zoning restrictions, and transmission infrastructure. To correct for the non-random sampling of alternatives, the log county inclusion probability enters the likelihood as an offset with its coefficient constrained to one.

Second, the estimated county-level parameters are used to calculate the deterministic utility Vijt∣mV_{ijt|m}Vijt∣m​ for each project-county pair and construct the inclusive value:

IV^imt=log⁡[∑j∈Jmexp⁡(V^ijt∣m)].(8.2.2.10)\widehat{IV}_{imt} = \log \left[ \sum_{j\in J_m} \exp(\widehat{V}_{ijt|m}) \right]. \tag{8.2.2.10}IVimt​=log​j∈Jm​∑​exp(Vijt∣m​)​.(8.2.2.10)

Third, the market-level random-coefficients logit is estimated using IV^imt\widehat{IV}_{imt}IVimt​ together with observed market characteristics:

Uimt=λiIV^imt+αm+Zmt′Θ+ωimt.(8.2.2.11)U_{imt} = \lambda_i\widehat{IV}_{imt} + \alpha_m + Z_{mt}'\Theta + \omega_{imt}. \tag{8.2.2.11}Uimt​=λi​IVimt​+αm​+Zmt′​Θ+ωimt​.(8.2.2.11)

Finally, because the inclusive value used in the market-choice model is a generated regressor from the county-choice estimation, standard errors are obtained using a bootstrap procedure that jointly resamples projects and markets. This propagates first-stage estimation uncertainty through the construction of inclusive values and into the market-level parameter estimates.

The estimated model is used to evaluate alternative Energy Community tax-credit designs. A change in eligibility or the subsidy rate changes county-level profitability, which affects county choice probabilities, inclusive values, and ultimately market choice probabilities.

where μi\mu_iμi​ converts utility changes into dollar values.
