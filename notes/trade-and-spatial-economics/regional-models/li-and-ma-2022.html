The Equilibrium

Given the parameters of the model, the equilibrium is defined as a vector of prices {wj,pj(ω)}\{w_j,p_j(\omega) \}{wj​,pj​(ω)}, a vector of quantities {qj(ω)}\{q_j(\omega) \}{qj​(ω)}, and a population distribution {Lj}\{L_j \}{Lj​} such that:

(1) Every individual maximizes his utility by choosing the location and the consumption bundle.

(2) Every firm maximizes its profit.

(3) The labor market in each location clears.

(4) Trade is balanced.
