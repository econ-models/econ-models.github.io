Model Setup

The economy consists of many trading counties, each indexed by ooo if the origin of a trade and by ddd if the destination. Agents in the model consume a continuum of differentiated goods varieties (indexed by jjj), and tastes over these varieties take a CES form (with elasticity σ\sigmaσ).
