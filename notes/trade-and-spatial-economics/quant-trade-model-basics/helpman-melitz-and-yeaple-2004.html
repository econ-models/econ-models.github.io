Reference: Helpman, Melitz and Yeaple (2004); Reading Notes

Helpman, Melitz and Yeaple (2004) is also an extremely influential paper in international economics. It models and distinguishes two different activities for multinational firms: Export and FDI. The core conclusion of this paper: firms will choose to serve local market, export, and invest directly from lowest productivity to highest productivity.

References

  • Helpman, Elhanan, Marc J Melitz, and Stephen R Yeaple. 2004. “Export versus FDI with heterogeneous firms.” American Economic Review, 94(1): 300–316.
