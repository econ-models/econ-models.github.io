Model Set-up

In this model, there is a finite number of countries i∈S≡{1,...,N}i\in S\equiv \{1,...,N \}i∈S≡{1,...,N}. There are a continuum of goods Ω\OmegaΩ, and every country is able to produce every good. Countries, however, vary (exogenously) in their productivity of each good; in particular, let zi(ω)z_i(\omega)zi​(ω) denote country iii's efficiency at producing good ω∈Ω\omega\in \Omegaω∈Ω. It is also assumed that all goods ω∈Ω\omega\in \Omegaω∈Ω are produced using the same bundle of inputs with a constant returns to scale technology. Let the cost of a bundle of inputs in country i∈Si\in Si∈S be cic_ici​ so that the cost of producing one unit of ω∈Ω\omega\in \Omegaω∈Ω in country i∈Si\in Si∈S is cizi(ω)\frac{c_i}{z_i(\omega)}zi​(ω)ci​​. Finally, suppose there is an iceberg trade cost τij≥1\tau_{ij}\geq 1τij​≥1 of trading a good from i∈Si\in Si∈S to j∈Sj\in Sj∈S.

Supply

The markets are assumed to be perfectly competitive, so the price is:

pij(ω)=cizi(ω)τij(6.1.3.1)p_{ij}(\omega)=\frac{c_i}{z_i(\omega)}\tau_{ij} \tag{6.1.3.1}pij​(ω)=zi​(ω)ci​​τij​(6.1.3.1)

Consumers in country j∈Sj\in Sj∈S are assumed to only purchase good ω∈Ω\omega\in \Omegaω∈Ω from the country who can provide it at the lowest price, so the price a consumer in j∈Sj\in Sj∈S actually pays for good ω∈Ω\omega\in\Omegaω∈Ω is:

pj(ω)≡min⁡i∈Spij(ω)=min⁡i∈Scizi(ω)τij(6.1.3.2)p_j(\omega)\equiv \mathop{\min}_{i\in S}p_{ij}(\omega)=\mathop{\min}_{i\in S}\frac{c_i}{z_i(\omega)}\tau_{ij} \tag{6.1.3.2}pj​(ω)≡mini∈S​pij​(ω)=mini∈S​zi​(ω)ci​​τij​(6.1.3.2)

In the model, the productivity zi(ω)z_i(\omega)zi​(ω) is treated as a random variable drawn independently and identically for each ω∈Ω\omega\in \Omegaω∈Ω. Define FiF_iFi​ to be the cumulative distribution function of the productivity in country i∈Si\in Si∈S. That is, for each i∈Si\in Si∈S, for all ω∈Ω\omega\in \Omegaω∈Ω:

Fi(z)≡Pr(zi(ω)≤z)(6.1.3.3)F_i(z)\equiv Pr(z_i(\omega)\leq z) \tag{6.1.3.3}Fi​(z)≡Pr(zi​(ω)≤z)(6.1.3.3)

In the model, it is assumed that Fi(z)F_i(z)Fi​(z) is Frechet distributed1 so that for all z≥0z\geq 0z≥0:
