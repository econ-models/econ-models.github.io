Model Setup

Suppose there is a compact set SSS of countries, and XijX_{ij}Xij​ is the trade from origin iii to destination jjj. The Armington assumption is that each country i∈Si\in Si∈S produces a distinct variety of a good. Suppose that each country i∈Si\in Si∈S is populated by a measure of LiL_iLi​ workers, which we assume is exogenous. Furthermore, let the productivity of a worker be AiA_iAi​, which we also assume is exogenous. Let the wage of a worker in country iii be wiw_iwi​. The wage will be determined in equilibrium. We assume that labor is the only factor of production.

Besides, there are three common assumptions made about the market structure: (1) markets in every country are perfectly competitive, so the price of a good is simply equal to its marginal cost; (2) each country is endowed with a certain quantity of a good and chooses the amount to sell to each destination to maximize profits; (3) production is monopolistically competitive.

Finally, suppose that there are iceberg trade costs τij\tau_{ij}τij​. This means that in order from one unit of a good to arrive in destination jjj, origin iii must ship τij\tau_{ij}τij​ units.

General Equilibrium

The basic Armington model is very easy. Given exogenous parameters {aij,τij,σ,Ai,Li}\{a_{ij},\tau_{ij},\sigma,A_i,L_i\}{aij​,τij​,σ,Ai​,Li​}, equilibrium is a vector of wages {wi}\{w_i\}{wi​}, such that final good and labor markets clear in all countries. The equilibrium is defined by a series of equations:

Yi=wiLi=∑j∈NλijwjLj=∑j∈Naij(τijwiAi)1−σ∑k∈Nakj(τkjwkAk)1−σwjLj=∑j∈Naij(τijwiAi)1−σ∑k∈Nakj(τkjwkAk)1−σYj(6.1.1.26)\begin{aligned} Y_i&=w_iL_i=\sum_{j\in N}\lambda_{ij}w_jL_j=\sum_{j\in N}\frac{a_{ij}(\frac{\tau_{ij}w_i}{A_i})^{1-\sigma}}{\sum_{k\in N}a_{kj}(\frac{\tau_{kj}w_k}{A_k})^{1-\sigma}}w_jL_j \\ &=\sum_{j\in N}\frac{a_{ij}(\frac{\tau_{ij}w_i}{A_i})^{1-\sigma}}{\sum_{k\in N}a_{kj}(\frac{\tau_{kj}w_k}{A_k})^{1-\sigma}}Y_j \end{aligned} \tag{6.1.1.26}Yi​​=wi​Li​=j∈N∑​λij​wj​Lj​=j∈N∑​∑k∈N​akj​(Ak​τkj​wk​​)1−σaij​(Ai​τij​wi​​)1−σ​wj​Lj​=j∈N∑​∑k∈N​akj​(Ak​τkj​wk​​)1−σaij​(Ai​τij​wi​​)1−σ​Yj​​(6.1.1.26)

In this equilibrium, we have NNN unknowns ({wi}\{w_i\}{wi​}) and NNN equations, so all unknowns (wages in all regions) can be solved and the equilibrium can be just identified.
