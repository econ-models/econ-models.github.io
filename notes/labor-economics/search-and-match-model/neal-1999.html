Viewed as a normative model for young workers, Neal's model tells them: don't shop for a firm until you have found a career you like. As a positive model, it predicts that workers will not switch careers after they have settled on one. Neal presents data indicating that while this stark prediction does not hold up perfectly, it is a good first approximation. He suggests that extending the model to include learning, along the lines of Jovanovic's model (Jovanovic 1979), could help explain the later career switches that his model misses.
