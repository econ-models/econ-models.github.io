Now let's close the model in a general equilibrium framework. Suppose consumers have Ramsey preference, we can derive the Euler Equation:

This final expression perfectly captures the three core insights of the Romer model:

(1) Scale effects (δH)(\delta H)(δH): The larger total human capital HHH is, the higher the growth rate ggg (the advantage of large economies).

(2) Returns to patience (ρ\rhoρ): A lower ρ\rhoρ (greater patience) leads to a higher growth rate ggg.

(3) Market distortions (Λ\LambdaΛ): Distortions arising from monopoly pricing are embedded in Λ\LambdaΛ, causing the equilibrium growth rate to be lower than the socially optimal growth rate.
