Policy Distortion

We will use τ\tauτ to generically refer to the establishment-level tax rate. Generically, we denote different specifications of policy by P(s,τ)P(s,\tau)P(s,τ) representing the probability that a establishment with productivity sss faces policy τ\tauτ. From the perspective of an entering establishment, it faces draws of sss and τ\tauτ, and what matters to them is the joint distribution over these pairs. For a given pdf hhh over the idiosyncratic draws of sss, different specifications of policy will induce different joint distributions over pairs of (s,τ)(s,\tau)(s,τ). We will represent this joint pdf by g(s,τ)g(s,\tau)g(s,τ).

A given distribution of establishment-level tax and subsidies need not lead to a balanced budget for the government. We assume that budget balance is achieved on a per-period basis by either lump-sum taxation or redistribution to the representative consumer. We denote the lump-sum tax by TtT_tTt​. Because our model does not have a labor/leisure decision, lump-sum taxes have no effect on the model's equilibrium.
