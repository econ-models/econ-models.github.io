Model Set-up

We consider an economy consisting of MMM regions (indexed by mmm), where production in each region follows a model similar to that proposed by Krugman et al. (1980), with free entry of firms. The total number of individuals is normalized to Lˉ=1\bar{L}=1Lˉ=1, and individuals make decisions regarding location and labor supply to maximize their utility. We incorporate flood risk into the model as follows. Let S={s1,s2,...}S=\{s_1,s_2,...\}S={s1​,s2​,...} denote the set of possible states of nature, where each state sss is characterized by a probability Pr(s)\text{Pr}(s)Pr(s) and a corresponding vector of flooding events, {ξ1(s),ξ2(s),...,ξM(s)}\{\xi_1(s),\xi_2(s),...,\xi_M(s)\}{ξ1​(s),ξ2​(s),...,ξM​(s)}, where binary variable ξm(s)∈{0,1}\xi_m(s)\in \{0,1\}ξm​(s)∈{0,1} indicates the occurrence of flooding. Following previous macro models on climate change, we consider actual flood events to affect firms' average productivity and workers' average amenities within each region, as well as destructing a portion of firms. Prior to the realization of shocks, individuals make decisions regarding location and labor supply, while firms make decisions regarding entry. Once shocks occur, production and consumption take place.
