CES production is the most popular production at the micro level. In a standard CES production function, σ\sigmaσ measures the difficulty to substitute different inputs (capital and labor) when their relative prices change. σ>1\sigma>1σ>1 implies capital and labor are substitutes, while 0<σ<10<\sigma<10<σ<1 means they are complementarities. For most micro firms, σ<1\sigma<1σ<1. We will talk about it more later in the Section Aggregation.
