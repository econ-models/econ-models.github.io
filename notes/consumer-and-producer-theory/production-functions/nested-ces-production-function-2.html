In some cases, the production function may be nested and extremely complex. A classical example is Acemoglu (2002), which has a 3-layer nested production function: (1) There is a unique final product YYY, whose market is perfectly competitive, and the production function is CES; (2) To produce the final product, we need intermediate goods YZY_ZYZ​ and YLY_LYL​, both of which are produced in perfectly competitive markets, and the production function is CES; (3) To produce the intermediate goods, we need some machines, which are provided by "technology monopolists".
