Density Function and Properties

The Pareto distribution is a probability distribution that describes a phenomenon where a small number of items account for the majority of an effect, famously known as the 80/20 rule. It is a power-law distribution characterized by a heavy tail, meaning that extreme values (e.g., very large incomes or wealth) are more common than they would be in a normal distribution. Where to Use

The Pareto distribution is often used in firm dynamics and urban economics models, including Kortum (1997), Weitzman (1998), Axtell (2001), and Jones (2023).
