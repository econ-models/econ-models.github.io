Basic Model

The Stone-Geary utility function is also called linear expenditure system (LES). It is essentially same as the Cobb-Douglas utility function, but has a minimum consumption level. Only the consumption above the minimum level can bring positive utility. The utility function has the form:

U=Πi(xi−ai)αi(2.2.3.1)U=\Pi_i(x_i-a_i)^{\alpha_i} \tag{2.2.3.1}U=Πi​(xi​−ai​)αi​(2.2.3.1)

subject to the budget constraint:

w=∑i=1npixi(2.2.3.2)w=\sum_{i=1}^np_ix_i \tag{2.2.3.2}w=i=1∑n​pi​xi​(2.2.3.2)

where aia_iai​ is the minimum consumption level for good nnn, such as the basic food supply. We also have:

∑iαi=1(2.2.3.3)\sum_{i}\alpha_i=1 \tag{2.2.3.3}i∑​αi​=1(2.2.3.3)

The Lagrangian is:

L=Πi(xi−ai)αi−λ(∑ipixi−w)(2.2.3.4)\mathcal{L}=\Pi_i(x_i-a_i)^{\alpha_i}-\lambda(\sum_{i}p_ix_i-w) \tag{2.2.3.4}L=Πi​(xi​−ai​)αi​−λ(i∑​pi​xi​−w)(2.2.3.4)

The F.O.C. on xix_ixi​ is:

∂L∂xi=αi(xi−ai)αi−1Πn≠i(xi−ai)αi−λpi=αiUxi−ai−λpi=0(2.2.3.5)\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial x_i}=\alpha_i(x_i-a_i)^{\alpha_i-1}\Pi_{n\neq i}(x_i-a_i)^{\alpha_i}-\lambda p_i=\alpha_i\frac{U}{x_i-a_i}-\lambda p_i=0 \tag{2.2.3.5}∂xi​∂L​=αi​(xi​−ai​)αi​−1Πn=i​(xi​−ai​)αi​−λpi​=αi​xi​−ai​U​−λpi​=0(2.2.3.5)

We can find:

xi=αiUλpi+ai(2.2.3.6)x_i=\frac{\alpha_i U}{\lambda p_i}+a_i \tag{2.2.3.6}xi​=λpi​αi​U​+ai​(2.2.3.6)

Similarly, we have:

xj=αjUλpj−aj(2.2.3.7)x_j=\frac{\alpha_j U}{\lambda p_j}-a_j \tag{2.2.3.7}xj​=λpj​αj​U​−aj​(2.2.3.7)

Then we have:

xi−aixj−aj=αiαjpjpi  ⇒  xi−ai=(αiαjpjpi)(xj−aj)(2.2.3.8)\frac{x_i-a_i}{x_j-a_j}=\frac{\alpha_i}{\alpha_j}\frac{p_j}{p_i}\;\Rightarrow\; x_i-a_i=\left(\frac{\alpha_i}{\alpha_j}\frac{p_j}{p_i}\right)(x_j-a_j) \tag{2.2.3.8}xj​−aj​xi​−ai​​=αj​αi​​pi​pj​​⇒xi​−ai​=(αj​αi​​pi​pj​​)(xj​−aj​)(2.2.3.8)

Recall the budget constraint:

w=∑ipixi(2.2.3.9)w=\sum_{i}p_ix_i \tag{2.2.3.9}w=i∑​pi​xi​(2.2.3.9)

Then we can write it as:

w=∑ipixi=∑ipi[(αiαjpjpi)(xj−aj)+ai]=∑i(αiαj)pjxj+∑ipiai=pjxjαj∑i=1nαi+∑ipiai=pjxjαj+∑ipiai(2.2.3.10)\begin{aligned} w&=\sum_{i}p_ix_i=\sum_{i}p_i\left[\left(\frac{\alpha_i}{\alpha_j}\frac{p_j}{p_i}\right)(x_j-a_j)+a_i\right] \\ &=\sum_{i}\left(\frac{\alpha_i}{\alpha_j}\right)p_jx_j+\sum_ip_ia_i\\ &=\frac{p_jx_j}{\alpha_j}\sum_{i=1}^n\alpha_i+\sum_ip_ia_i\\ &=\frac{p_jx_j}{\alpha_j}+\sum_ip_ia_i \end{aligned} \tag{2.2.3.10}w​=i∑​pi​xi​=i∑​pi​[(αj​αi​​pi​pj​​)(xj​−aj​)+ai​]=i∑​(αj​αi​​)pj​xj​+i∑​pi​ai​=αj​pj​xj​​i=1∑n​αi​+i∑​pi​ai​=αj​pj​xj​​+i∑​pi​ai​​(2.2.3.10)

It means that the consumer will first consume the minimum consumption level of each good iii and spend w−∑ipiaiw-\sum_ip_ia_iw−∑i​pi​ai​, and then spend the remaining money on different goods as Cobb-Douglas utility function.

Therefore, we have the Marshallian demand for good jjj:

xj=aj+αjpj(w−∑ipiai)(2.2.3.11)x_j=a_j+\frac{\alpha_j}{p_j}(w-\sum_ip_ia_i) \tag{2.2.3.11}xj​=aj​+pj​αj​​(w−i∑​pi​ai​)(2.2.3.11)

The indirect utility is:

U=Πi(xi−ai)αi=Πi[αipi(w−∑jpjaj)]αi(2.2.3.12)U=\Pi_i(x_i-a_i)^{\alpha_i}=\Pi_i\left[\frac{\alpha_i}{p_i}(w-\sum_jp_ja_j)\right]^{\alpha_i} \tag{2.2.3.12}U=Πi​(xi​−ai​)αi​=Πi​[pi​αi​​(w−j∑​pj​aj​)]αi​(2.2.3.12)
