Model Setup

Modern monopolistic competition model is developed by Dixit and Stiglitz (1977), and then became the foundation of a series models like Krugman et al. (1980). The main features include: (1) many buyers and sellers; (2) product differentiation; (3) free entry and exit (zero economic profits); (4) some degree of market power (markup in pricing). In a monopolistic competition, we also need a fixed cost.
