Model Setup

BLP model is a type mixed logit model developed by Berry, Levinsohn and Pakes (1995), with several special features: (1) BLP model emphasized the endogeneity of price, and uses IV strategy to deal with the endogeneity; (2) BLP model uses market share data rather than individual-level choice data; (3) BLP model adopts GMM estimation, rather than SML estimation.
